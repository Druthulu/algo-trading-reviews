2 · The 99.9%-cash illusion

Pooled across the majors. A high Sharpe on an account that is almost always in cash says nothing about trade quality — and "beating" a −44%-median buy-and-hold is just not being in the market.

3 · Alpha vs. BTC-beta (market-neutral test)

Whole-period regression on BTC (Newey-West). S_full's +1.3%/yr at zero beta is real in-sample — but it's smaller than the no-gate ladder's, so the RSI gate subtracts alpha; and it's the fee for writing naked crash-insurance, which one tail event erases.

4 · The drawdown is small only because the tail never fired

The reported 2.21% is a closed-trade number. In-sample zero trades closed at a loss across 13 coins (several down 70–90%) — the ladder always escaped on a bounce. Feed it a smooth −56% decline with no bounce and the trade loses 46%.

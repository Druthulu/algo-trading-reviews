Alpha vs. beta — whole period

Factor-neutral annualized alpha (Fama-French 5-factor, Dimson-lagged market term, Newey-West standard errors) plotted against market beta — one point per universe, per book. Unlike the per-year bars above, these are single whole-period regressions. ATD is the only book below the zero-alpha line: negative alpha at moderate beta. The EMA-200 control sits just above zero; buy-and-hold carries the market (beta ≈ 1) with mildly positive alpha.

Each panel is one universe. Bars are calendar-year values for the ATD book, the EMA-200 in/out control (long when price > its trailing 200-day EMA, else flat), and equal-weight buy-and-hold — all through the same engine, same costs, same dividends. The control is the yardstick: if the cascade's flags, TRIX and pillars added timing skill, ATD would beat it. It does not, in any universe or cost tier.
Pre-registered before running (decision rule fixed in advance). Primary metric = paired block-bootstrap ΔSharpe of ATD − EMA-200 control at 3 bps; negative in all 4 universes (p_gt0 ≤ 0.20). Method and fixes are summarized in the accompanying write-up.
